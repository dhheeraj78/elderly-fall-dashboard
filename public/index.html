Privacy Policy & Biometric Data Safety

Effective Date: October 2026 | Version 2.4-ESP32 Compliance

1. On-Device Cryptographic Architecture

ElderCare IoT prioritizes end-to-end patient confidentiality. All sensitive biometric vitals, fall alerts, and GPS coordinates are authenticated and encrypted directly on the ESP32 microcontroller using the NIST-selected ASCON-128 AEAD lightweight cryptographic suite before transmission to cloud relays.

2. Zero Storage of Raw Biometric Audio/Video

Our wearable system does not record, stream, or retain raw audio, microphone streams, or optical camera footage. The MPU6050 and MAX30102 sensors measure purely rotational acceleration, angular velocity, and optical PPG reflection ratios.

3. Client-Side Telemetry Caching

Personal patient preferences (such as patient name and emergency contacts) are stored strictly within the client browser's local sandbox (localStorage) and are never monetized or shared with third-party advertising networks.

4. Incident Reporting & Emergency Dispatch

When a confirmed fall impact occurs, encrypted packets containing coordinate tags are securely transmitted to authorized caregivers and designated emergency hotlines.

Terms & Assistive Telemetry Guidelines

1. Non-Diagnostic Assistive Purpose

The ElderCare IoT Wearable Platform is an assistive, research-grade remote monitoring solution designed to provide caregivers with real-time awareness. It does not constitute a certified medical diagnostic device or replacement for professional clinical care.

2. Network & GPS Availability

Transmission of emergency notifications depends upon active cellular, Wi-Fi, and satellite GNSS connectivity. In rural or enclosed environments, GPS accuracy may vary.

3. Emergency Services Protocol

In life-threatening emergencies, caregivers should immediately contact official regional emergency hotlines (e.g., 911 / 112) rather than relying exclusively on automated wireless pings.
